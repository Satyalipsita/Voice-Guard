What the detector hears

Longer bars point toward a synthetic voice.

Pitch movement-

Real speech rises and falls. Clones are often too steady.

Pitch micro-variation-

Natural voices wobble slightly from moment to moment.

Loudness dynamics-

People vary stress and volume; generated speech is often even.

High-frequency detail-

Breath and fricatives add energy above 7 kHz.

Background noise in pauses-

Real rooms never go fully silent.

This is a signal-based screening tool, not proof. Phone compression, noise suppression and monotone speakers can raise the score, and a high-quality clone can lower it. A production detector would add a trained deep-learning model. Audio is analysed in your browser and never uploaded.
